# Privacy Policy for Universal Volume Guard

**Last Updated:** [Date of Publication]

Thank you for choosing Universal Volume Guard ("the extension"). Your privacy is our top priority. This policy outlines our principles regarding data handling.

### 1. The Core Principle: No Data Collection

Universal Volume Guard is designed to operate entirely on your local computer. We believe that your browsing and listening habits are your own.

Therefore, the extension **does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information or any other type of user data.**

### 2. How Audio Processing Works

The core function of the extension is to analyze and adjust the volume of audio playing in your browser. This analysis is performed in real-time, exclusively within your browser.

*   **Local Analysis:** All audio data is processed locally. It is never sent to our servers or any third-party service.
*   **Ephemeral Processing:** The audio data is processed "in the moment" and is immediately discarded. It is never saved to your hard drive or any other storage.

### 3. Permissions

The extension requires the `storage` permission in your browser. This permission is used **only** to save your personal settings for the extension, such as whether it is enabled and your chosen target loudness. This data is stored locally in your browser's storage area and is not accessible to us.

### 4. No Third-Party Services

The extension does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

### 5. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, we will inform users through the Chrome Web Store listing. However, our core principle of not collecting user data will not change.
