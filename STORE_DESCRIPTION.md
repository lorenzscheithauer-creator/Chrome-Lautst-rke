## Full Description

### Stop the Volume Madness!

Ever jumped out of your seat when an ad suddenly blares at full volume? Or struggled to hear quiet dialogue in a movie, only to be deafened by the next action scene? Universal Volume Guard is the solution.

This lightweight, powerful extension works silently in the background to create a perfectly balanced and consistent listening experience across your entire browser. It intelligently analyzes the loudness of any audio or video content in real-time and gently adjusts it to a comfortable, steady level.

### How It Works

*   **Smart Loudness Detection:** Using the professional LUFS loudness standard (the same one used by major platforms like YouTube and Spotify), the extension measures perceived loudness, not just peak volume.
*   **Seamless Adjustments:** It automatically turns down overly loud content and boosts quiet audio, ensuring you never have to reach for the volume knob again.
*   **Universal Compatibility:** Works on any site that uses HTML5 audio or video, including YouTube, Twitch, Spotify, Netflix, and news sites.
*   **High-Quality & Performant:** All audio processing is done with high-quality components that prevent distortion and artifacts. It's built to be extremely efficient and won't slow down your browser.

### Key Features:

*   Enable or disable normalization with a single click.
*   Customize the target loudness to your exact preference using a simple slider.

### Your Privacy is Paramount

Universal Volume Guard respects your privacy completely. All audio analysis happens locally on your computer, in real-time. The extension **does not collect, store, or transmit any data whatsoever.** Your listening habits remain your own.

Install Universal Volume Guard today and enjoy a smoother, safer, and more pleasant audio experience on the web!
